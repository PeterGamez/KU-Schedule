Privacy Policy for KU Schedule - ตารางเรียน มก.

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 | Last Updated: November 21, 2026
Core Commitment: KU Schedule is an open-source, 100% client-side Chrome Extension. We do not operate external servers, and we never collect, store, sell, or track your personal information or university schedule data.

1. Overview

KU Schedule - ตารางเรียน มก. ("the Extension") is an unofficial student project designed for Kasetsart University students to retrieve, format, and organize class timetables from the official university portal (my.ku.th) into weekly visual calendars, downloadable PNG images, and Google Calendar events.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): The Extension never collects student IDs, passwords, names, or contact details.
  • No External Servers: All timetable data is processed directly inside your browser. No data is ever sent to developer servers or third-party databases.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The Extension contains zero telemetry, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies.
  • Google API User Data Policy Compliance: The Extension's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Google Calendar Integration (OAuth 2.0)

When you explicitly use the "Sync to Google Calendar" feature:

  • Authentication: The Extension connects to Google via Chrome's official chrome.identity OAuth 2.0 API.
  • Scopes Used: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events
  • Purpose: These permissions are strictly used to create a dedicated secondary calendar (e.g. ตารางเรียน มก.) and insert your class events with weekly recurrences.
  • Zero Data Access: We never read, modify, or collect your personal calendar events, emails, or contacts.

4. Local Storage (chrome.storage.local)

To provide offline viewing and remember user settings, minimal data is cached locally on your device:

  • Schedule Cache: Temporarily caches your timetable for offline access.
  • Preferences: Saves your chosen language (Thai/English) and calendar title format.

This data stays on your machine and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the extension.

5. Permissions Justification

  • host_permissions (https://my.ku.th/*): To fetch course data from your active session on the university portal.
  • host_permissions (https://calendar.google.com/*, https://www.googleapis.com/*): To sync events directly to Google Calendar.
  • identity: To authenticate securely with Google OAuth 2.0.
  • storage: To store offline schedule cache and user preferences locally.
  • scripting: To safely render schedule capture areas.
